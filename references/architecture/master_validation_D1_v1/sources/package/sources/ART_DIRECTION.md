# Art Direction

Use **RastalR Everyday Pixel**, adapted as warm clinical pixel art: medium-detail, hard-edged, contemporary, readable, and slightly stylized. It must work in RPGs, life sims, management games, and restrained horror variants.

Sprites use a rectangular-grid RPG oblique view: no diamond/isometric grid, no 45-degree axes, horizontal and vertical construction edges remain horizontal/vertical, and depth recedes upward. Default freestanding anchor is bottom-center. Validate scale against a 26 x 46 px adult.

Outline in navy-charcoal (`#182331`, `#273746`, sparingly `#111923`), favoring the outer silhouette. Light is always upper-left. Use controlled warm ivory, steel/slate, muted teal/surgical green, wood/beige, lavender, pediatric yellow, and sparse emergency red-orange/amber. Keep backgrounds transparent, highlights mostly unoutlined, and shadows limited to compact contact shadows.

Materials: plastic is broad and softly contrasted; metal/stainless has cool sharper highlights and recessed joints; glass is muted blue-gray with sparse gleams; fabric uses 2–3 gentle values; screens have dark housings and abstract display marks. Do not use blur, anti-aliasing, heavy dithering, text generated by AI, logos, or large cast shadows.
